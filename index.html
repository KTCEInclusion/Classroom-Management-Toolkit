1. The Discipline Breakdown

Click any card below to explore the core rule:

🤝 Building Relationships 40%

Connect before you correct. Learn student names fast, show genuine interest, and give 5 seconds of wait-time when calling on hesitant students.

👁️ ProximityMoving physically closer to students to manage behavior quietly. Monitoring 25%

Keep moving! Walk near students who are losing focus instead of stopping your lesson to call them out across the room.

🚦 Clear Expectations 25%

Don't just announce rules—teach and practice them together. Show students exactly what proper behavior looks like in action.

⚖️ Fair Consequences 10%

Praise in public, correct in private. Always protect a student's dignitySelf-respect and pride; avoiding public embarrassment. to prevent power struggles.
