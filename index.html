🎓 Classroom Management Toolkit

Complete 5-Module Program

Practical, everyday strategies for supportive & effective teaching.

Module 1

Building Strong Relationships (40% Rule)

Connect before you correct. Students respond best when they know you care.

🤝 Key Strategy: The 5-Second Wait

When calling on a hesitant student, pause for 5 full seconds. Giving up too fast shows low confidence in them. Waiting shows you believe they can succeed!

🧘 Teacher Cool-Down

When frustration builds, take a 30-second reset: assign a quick silent reading task or take 3 deep breaths before responding.

Module 2

Classroom Setup & Clear Rules

Prevention is better than correction. Set clear boundaries before problems start.

Rule Check: Teach vs. Announce

❌ Announcing: "Be good today!" (Too vague)
🟢 Teaching: "Watch how I raise my hand quietly, then let's practice together."
